# 🛒 E-commerce Marketing & Sales Analysis

> **A data-driven exploration of customer acquisition, retention, revenue, product performance, and customer lifetime value.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The e-commerce company aims to leverage **data-driven insights** to improve customer acquisition, strengthen customer retention, and optimize revenue.

This project analyzes sales, customer, product, marketing, and transactional data to identify important business trends and answer key questions related to **customer behavior, revenue generation, product performance, segmentation, retention, and seasonality**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this analysis is to understand:

- 📈 How customer acquisition and retention change over time
- 💰 How revenue is generated by new and existing customers
- 🛍️ Which products and categories perform best
- 👥 Which customer segments contribute the most revenue
- 🔄 How customer retention changes across cohorts
- 💎 How customer lifetime value varies by acquisition period
- 📅 How sales patterns change by month, day, category, and location

---

# 🔎 Business Questions

### 1. 📈 Customer Acquisition Trends

Identify the months with the **highest and lowest customer acquisition counts**.

**Business Question:**  
What strategies could be implemented to address acquisition fluctuations and ensure consistent growth throughout the year?

---

### 2. 🔄 Customer Retention

Analyze customer behavior during **high-retention months**.

**Business Question:**  
What patterns can be identified during successful retention periods, and how can these strategies be replicated throughout the year?

---

### 3. 💰 New vs Existing Customer Revenue

Compare the revenue generated by **new and existing customers** month-over-month.

**Business Question:**  
What does this trend suggest about the balance between customer acquisition and retention efforts?

---

### 4. 🛍️ Top-Performing Products

Identify the **top-performing products** and analyze the factors contributing to their success.

**Business Question:**  
How can these insights support inventory management and promotional strategies?

---

### 5. 👥 Customer Segmentation — RFM Analysis

Segment customers into:

- 🏆 Premium
- 🥇 Gold
- 🥈 Silver
- 📊 Standard

using **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis**.

**Business Question:**  
What targeted strategies can be developed for each customer segment to improve retention and revenue?

---

### 6. 💎 Revenue Contribution by Customer Segment

Analyze the revenue contribution of each customer segment.

**Business Question:**  
How can the company focus its efforts on high-value customer segments while continuing to nurture lower-value segments?

---

### 7. 📊 Cohort Retention Analysis

Group customers according to their **month of first purchase** and analyze retention rates over time.

**Business Questions:**

- Which cohorts demonstrate the highest retention?
- Which cohorts demonstrate the lowest retention?
- What strategies could improve retention among weaker cohorts?

---

### 8. 💰 Customer Lifetime Value

Analyze the **lifetime value (LTV)** of customers acquired in different months.

**Business Question:**  
How can customer lifetime value insights inform acquisition and retention strategies?

---

### 9. 🌦️ Seasonal Sales Trends

Analyze seasonal sales patterns by:

- Product category
- Location
- Time period

**Business Question:**  
How can the company prepare for peak and off-peak seasons to maximize revenue?

---

### 10. 📅 Daily Sales Trends

Analyze daily sales performance to identify:

- High-performing days
- Low-performing days
- Recurring sales patterns

**Business Question:**  
What strategies could be implemented to improve sales performance on slower days?

---
